// the mixtape engine _

term krush

Line up your tracks, tap the beat once per song, hit render. TermKrush mixes them into one continuous old-school scratch mix — one tempo, seamless cuts, scratches and bass drops placed like a human. Same input, same mix, every time.

01
sequence

Drop your tracks on one line, in the order you want — the same song can come back around. That's the whole project.

02
tap

Tap along to each track once. A least-squares fit turns your taps into its exact tempo and downbeat, cached forever.

03
krush

A deterministic engine does the craft: one master tempo, phrase-perfect swaps, whip/wiki scratches, bass drops on the one.
