## Goal
Navigate one level of folders in the Library.

## Spec
- On a subfolder, `Enter` or `Right` shows its contents; `Esc` or `Left` goes back.
- `Insert` on a folder renames it (same as a file).
- Strictly one level — sub-sub-folders are not allowed.
