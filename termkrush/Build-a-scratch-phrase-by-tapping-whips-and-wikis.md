## Problem
The user composes a phrase by tapping a rhythm of whips/wikis (e.g. "whip whip wiki-whip"), recorded onto the scratch pad and quantized to tempo. Combos: wiki-whip, whip-wiki.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Tap whip/wiki in time; the sequence is captured as the pad's scratch phrase, quantized to the grid.
- [ ] Combos (wiki-whip / whip-wiki) are expressible.
- [ ] Triggering the pad plays the stored phrase; engine test on a known tap sequence.
