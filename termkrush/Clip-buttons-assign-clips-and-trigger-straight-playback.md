## Intent
A bank of clip buttons (slots). Assign a recorded clip to a button, then trigger it to play once (straight) over the mix.
## Acceptance
- [ ] Recorded clips assign to numbered buttons; each button shows filled/empty + a label.
- [ ] Triggering a button plays its clip over the deck mix (one-shot voice); polyphonic.
- [ ] Empty button is a safe no-op; tests cover assign + audible trigger.
