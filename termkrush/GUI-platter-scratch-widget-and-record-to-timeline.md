## Goal
The GUI platter + recording the scratch to the timeline (the front-end half of scratching). Absorbs the old "scratch not discoverable" bug.
## Scope
- Platter/waveform widget: **mouse-drag left/right = whip/wiki**, drag speed = velocity (drives the #6 jog voice); arrow keys as a secondary quantized jog ("Both").
- While timeline-recording, capture the jog performance as a block at the cued position.
- Visible playhead / scratch feedback; discoverable (it's a draggable platter, no hidden cue).
