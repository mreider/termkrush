## Problem statement

Scratching usually targets a short window of audio (a stab, a vocal). Need to mark in and out.

## Possible solution

- Square brackets on focused deck (in scratch-arm mode) mark in / out points.
- Visualized as a highlighted segment on the position bar.
- Esc clears the zone.

## Acceptance

- [ ] In/out marks land at the current playhead.
- [ ] Highlighted segment appears between marks.
- [ ] Clearing removes the highlight.
